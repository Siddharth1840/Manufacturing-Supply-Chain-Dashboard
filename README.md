🔍 Project Overview
———————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
This project focuses on analyzing manufacturing production and supply chain data to uncover cost patterns, workforce distribution, and warehouse efficiency. Built in Power BI, the dashboard provides a unified view of how production cost, product quantity, employee distribution, and warehouse storage influence overall manufacturing performance.

🎯 Objectives
———————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
➡ Identify trends in production cost and quantity across product types and countries.

➡ Measure warehouse-wise storage distribution to support inventory planning.

➡ Analyze employee distribution across departments and its link to operational cost.

➡ Track year-over-year cost and quantity trends to support forecasting.

➡ Provide data-driven insights to enhance manufacturing decision-making.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
———————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
➡ Power BI – Dashboard development & data visualization

➡ DAX – Advanced calculations & KPIs

📈 Key Insights
———————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————
➡ Cost Efficiency Trends: Total cost dropped from $3.67M (2023) to $2.42M (2025), while quantity stayed relatively stable — indicating improved production  
   efficiency over time.

➡ Product Type Cost Analysis: Automobiles and Textiles carry the highest average production cost (~$10.6K), while Machinery is the most cost-efficient 
   (~$9.9K).

➡ Country-Wise Cost Contribution: The USA (21.2%) and Germany (~20%) lead in production cost share, closely followed by India, China, and Japan.

➡ Warehouse Distribution: Storage is fairly balanced across regions, with the West warehouse (26.17%) holding the largest share of total quantity.

➡ Workforce Structure: Support functions — HR, Logistics, and Quality — together make up nearly 58% of the workforce, notably higher than core Production 
   staff (18.88%).
